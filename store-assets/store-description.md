ReadMate is an elegant, privacy-friendly text-to-speech (TTS) and immersive reading companion designed for language learners, researchers, and daily web readers.

Listen to articles with high-definition neural voices, read along with synchronized word highlighting, or eliminate online distractions with our full-screen Reader Mode. You can even import your own books and turn them into audiobooks.

🆕 What's New in v1.0.9: Read Your Own Books (Book Page)
- Import txt, Markdown, EPUB and PDF files and read them like a real book, with a library, automatic reading progress and six eye-care themes.
- EPUB files are unpacked natively (EPUB3 + EPUB2 table of contents); scripts, styles, images, footnotes and ruby phonetics are stripped so narration stays clean.
- PDFs are reflowed from their text layer by a built-in offline engine, so they can be read aloud, highlighted and translated. Scanned PDFs without a text layer are reported clearly instead of silently failing.
- Right-click any .epub / .pdf link on the web to send it straight to the Book Page.
- Export a print-ready PDF or a standalone bilingual HTML file in one click.
- Clicking a paragraph now shows a small confirmation bubble before playback starts, and reading always begins from the exact sentence you clicked.

🌟 Key Features:

1. 📖 Immersive Reader Mode
Transform cluttered web pages into a clean, book-like reading layout with soothing themes (Vintage Sepia, Dark, E-Ink, etc.). Enjoy sentence-by-sentence read-along highlighting, PDF export, and one-click MP3 audiobook downloads.

2. 📚 Book Page - Read Your Own Books
Import local txt / Markdown / EPUB / PDF books into a dedicated reading page. Read them exactly like web articles: narration, click-to-read, dictionary lookup, bilingual translation and export all work the same way.

3. ☁️ Natural Voices & Neural TTS
Supports crystal-clear neural speech with natural pacing and emotion. Choose between local browser voices for zero latency or cloud neural voices for supreme naturalness.

4. 🌐 Bilingual Streaming & Audio Subtitles
Switch seamlessly between original audio, translated audio, or alternate bilingual playback. Live bilingual floating subtitles keep you on track without losing context.

5. ⚡ Instant Vocabulary & AI Highlights
Hover or select words to view instant definitions, phonetic transcriptions, and save them to your personal notebook. Trigger AI summaries to distill thousands of words into bilingual bullet points in seconds.

🔒 Privacy First:
- Zero Data Collection: Your reading preferences and vocabulary stay strictly in your local browser storage.
- Offline Book Parsing: EPUB and PDF files are parsed entirely inside your browser - no book is ever uploaded to any server.
- Ad-Free & Distraction-Free: Clean and lightweight experience with no promotional clutter.

ReadMate（读伴）是一款为深度阅读者、外语学习者打造的纯净网页朗读与 AI 辅助阅读扩展。

无论您是在阅读外语新闻、技术博客、学术论文，还是想在做家务、通勤时“听”网页，读伴都能提供如丝般顺滑的声画同步体验。现在，它还能读您自己的电子书。

🆕 v1.0.9 新功能：读伴书页 —— 自己的书也能读
- 支持导入 txt / Markdown / epub / PDF 四种书稿当书读，带书库管理、阅读进度自动记忆与六套护眼主题；
- epub 原生解包（EPUB3 / EPUB2 两套目录都认），脚本、样式、图片、脚注与注音拼音自动剔除，朗读干净；
- PDF 由内置离线引擎逐页抽取文字层并重排，能朗读、能划词、能双语；扫描版（无文字层）会明确提示报错，绝不假装成功；
- 网页上遇到 .epub / .pdf 链接，右键即可直接送进书页；
- 一键导出打印级 PDF 与独立双语 HTML；
- 单击正文先弹气泡确认后再开播，且永远从您点的那一句开始读。

🌟 核心功能亮点：

1. 📖 墨阅级“沉浸净读模式”（Reader Mode）
一键滤除网页杂乱广告与侧边栏干扰，以典雅全屏书页（提供羊皮纸、夜间黑、护眼绿等护眼底色）呈现纯净正文。支持字体字号调节、声画双层高亮跟读、一键排版导出纯净 PDF，更支持整篇打包下载为 MP3 有声书！

2. 📚 读伴书页 —— 整本书也能读
把本地 txt / Markdown / epub / PDF 电子书导入专属书页，像读网页一样地读它：朗读、点读、查词、双语翻译与导出，全部一视同仁。

3. ☁️ 高品质自然语音朗读（TTS）
内置高质量自然语音支持，支持拟真情感发音，亦可调用浏览器本地原生语音（零延迟运行）；同时兼容通用标准语音接口，随心切换。

4. 🌐 双轨对照与自由听读
支持【仅听原文】、【外文直接读译文】、【双语对照交替朗读】三档模式。在控制条优雅呈现双语流动字幕，满足自学外语的精听精读需求。

5. ⚡ 毫秒级极速查词 + AI 核心要点提炼
遇到生词鼠标轻触即显音标与权威释义，支持一键加入生词本导出；点击摘要按钮，大模型可在数秒内为您提炼全篇 4~6 条中英双语核心要点，快速掌握文章主旨。

🔒 隐私与品质承诺：
- 零数据收集：所有阅读设置、生词仅保存在您的本地浏览器中。
- 书稿离线解析：epub / PDF 完全在您的浏览器内部解析，绝不把您的书上传到任何服务器。
- 无广告干扰：纯净独立开发，绝不插入推广内容与多余弹窗。
- 轻量顺畅：原生代码驱动，秒开不卡顿，极低资源占用。
